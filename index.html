Paid Advertising Should Never Feel Like a Black Box

Sigma's process is designed to make campaign delivery transparent, responsibilities clear, and optimization decisions understandable. Clients should always know four things.

01

What Is Happening

Significant changes, approvals, and recommendations are documented, and reports explain what happened and why it matters, not just the numbers.

02

What Is Needed From You

Business and offer details, account access, budget limits, timely approvals, quick responses to enquiries, and sales feedback. These responsibilities are agreed during onboarding.

03

How Performance Is Evaluated

Advertising-platform metrics are considered alongside lead quality and sales feedback whenever those records are available, because platform data is not automatically confirmed revenue or profit.

Six Stages, From Onboarding to Reporting

Every engagement follows the same structure. Each stage shows what Sigma handles, what the client handles, and the outcome you can expect, so nothing is left unclear.

01

Onboarding & Business Assessment

First, we understand the business before spending the budget. We gather what we need to build a campaign around the client's offer, audience, goals, sales process, budget, and operational capacity.

  • Sigma handles: business assessment, campaign requirements, access checklist, initial questions, and flagging risks or missing information
  • Client provides: business and offer details, target market, assets, ad account access, budget limits, and sales-process details
  • Outcome: an agreed campaign brief, clear responsibilities, and outstanding requirements
02

Strategy & Campaign Planning

Every campaign has a defined objective and a reason behind its decisions. The plan covers audience, offer, messaging, creative direction, conversion journey, budget allocation, tracking requirements, and performance indicators, and names the main assumptions to validate.

  • Sigma handles: strategy development, campaign structure, creative recommendations, measurement planning, and initial testing priorities
  • Client handles: reviewing the approach, confirming business details, and approving the budget and scope
  • Outcome: an agreed campaign strategy and implementation plan
03

Creative Development & Approval

No campaign goes live with unapproved messaging or an unclear offer. Depending on scope, Sigma prepares or coordinates images, videos, headlines, primary text, calls to action, and destination-page recommendations. The client reviews them for business accuracy, pricing, product details, brand consistency, and offer terms.

  • Sigma handles: preparing campaign materials, explaining creative choices, and making agreed revisions
  • Client handles: providing accurate information, reviewing deliverables, and approving final materials within the agreed timeframe
  • Outcome: approved advertising materials ready for implementation
04

Campaign Setup & Launch

We check the important details before activating the campaign. Sigma configures the agreed objective, audience approach, budget, placements, creative, destination, and available tracking, then checks the setup against the approved plan. The campaign launches once access, assets, approvals, and funding are in place.

  • Sigma handles: campaign implementation, pre-launch checks, and launch confirmation
  • Client handles: a funded ad account, required access, and an offer that can be fulfilled
  • Outcome: a live campaign with a defined objective, measurement plan, and monitoring process
05

Monitoring & Optimization

Launching is the beginning of the work, not the end. Sigma monitors delivery and available performance data for trends, technical issues, creative opportunities, and bottlenecks. Depending on the evidence, decisions may include testing new creative, adjusting budget allocation, investigating audience response, improving the conversion path, or limiting spend on weak approaches.

  • Sigma handles: performance monitoring, identifying issues, documenting decisions, and implementing agreed improvements
  • Client handles: responding to enquiries, timely sales feedback, reporting lead outcomes, and flagging changes to pricing, availability, or fulfilment
  • Outcome: evidence-informed adjustments and a record of what is being learned
06

Reporting & Next Steps

Every report should explain what happened, why it matters, and what comes next. Performance updates are provided at the agreed reporting frequency and tailored to the campaign objective and the data available.

  • Sigma handles: reporting performance, explaining decisions, identifying bottlenecks, and recommending next steps
  • Client handles: confirming sales outcomes, sharing relevant business data, raising concerns, and approving decisions that need client approval
  • Outcome: a shared understanding of performance and a clear plan for the next stage

Communication, Approvals and Accountability

To keep delivery organized, responsibilities and communication expectations are agreed during onboarding. Here is who handles what.

• Strategy

Sigma develops and explains the proposed campaign. The client reviews and approves the plan.

• Creative

Sigma prepares the agreed advertising materials. The client verifies business accuracy and approves the final assets.

• Budget & Major Changes

Sigma recommends allocation, manages agreed spend settings, and explains the rationale for major changes, seeking approval where required. The client approves the budget, funds the ad account, and approves changes outside the agreed scope or budget.

• Campaign Management

Sigma implements, monitors, and optimizes the campaign. The client provides access and reports operational changes.

• Enquiry Handling

Sigma advises on conversion tracking and follow-up requirements. The client responds to leads and manages sales.

• Reporting

Sigma shares available performance data, findings, and next steps. The client provides lead-quality and sales feedback.

Reporting: What Every Report Should Explain

Reports are tailored to the campaign objective and the data available. Sigma distinguishes observed results from interpretations and unverified assumptions.

Depending on the Engagement, Reports May Cover:

  • ✓ Spend, reach, impressions, clicks, and relevant engagement metrics.
  • ✓ Enquiries, purchases, cost per lead, or other primary conversion measures.
  • ✓ Lead quality, qualified opportunities, and sales outcomes where client records are available.
  • ✓ Tests conducted, findings, limitations, and outstanding questions.
  • ✓ Actions taken, recommended changes, and priorities for the next period.

Reports Will NOT:

  • ✕ Treat advertising-platform data as automatically equal to confirmed revenue or profit.
  • ✕ Blur observed results with interpretations and unverified assumptions.
  • ✕ Present numbers without context about why they matter.
  • ✕ Recommend scaling without a justification.

Communication and Approval Standards

Simple standards that keep delivery organized and every decision traceable.

Communication

One channel, agreed up front.

  • One agreed communication channel, with relevant contacts set during onboarding
  • Reporting frequency and review meetings, where applicable, confirmed before launch

Approvals & Budget

Nothing material happens without sign-off.

  • Campaigns, creative materials, and material changes are submitted for approval before implementation
  • Sigma operates within the approved budget; material increases or out-of-scope spend need client authorization

Escalation & Records

Problems surfaced early, decisions written down.

  • Tracking failures, account restrictions, unusual spending, or other material problems are communicated when identified
  • Significant changes, approvals, and recommendations are recorded in the agreed system

Response times, reporting frequency, revision limits, and service scope are agreed for each engagement rather than assumed to be identical for every client. Ad spend itself is paid directly to Meta, TikTok, or Google, separate from our management fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

How working with Sigma is organized, from onboarding to reporting.

What do you need from me at the start? +

Business and offer details, your target market, relevant assets, ad account access, budget limits, and details of your sales process. From these, Sigma prepares an agreed campaign brief, clear responsibilities, and a list of any outstanding requirements.

Will anything go live without my approval? +

No. Campaigns, creative materials, and material changes requiring approval are submitted before implementation, and a campaign launches only once the required access, assets, approvals, and funding are in place.

Who controls the budget? +

You do. You approve the budget and fund the ad account. Sigma recommends the allocation and manages agreed spend settings within the approved budget, and any material increase or out-of-scope expenditure requires your authorization.

How often will I get reports, and what will they say? +

Reporting frequency is agreed for each engagement before launch. Reports explain what happened, why it matters, and what comes next, tailored to the campaign objective and the data available.

Is advertising-platform data the same as my revenue? +

No. Platform data is not automatically equivalent to confirmed revenue or profit. That is why Sigma asks for lead-quality and sales feedback, and why reports separate observed results from interpretations and unverified assumptions.

What happens if something goes wrong? +

Tracking failures, account restrictions, unusual spending, or other material problems are communicated when identified, and significant changes and decisions are recorded in the agreed communication or reporting system.

Do you guarantee sales or ROAS? +

No honest advertising agency promises guaranteed sales, because ad platforms operate on auction dynamics and results depend on product demand, customer service speed, and fulfillment. What we control is the process: a testing framework that shuts down unprofitable ads quickly and scales winning angles systematically, to protect your budget.

How much does working with Sigma cost? +

Management fees depend on scope, the channels involved, campaign complexity, and how much ongoing management your business needs. We quote this transparently after an initial conversation about your business. Your ad spend is paid directly to Meta, TikTok, or Google and is always separate from our fee.
